Defense Strategies

Verify identities

Confirm who you are speaking with via an official channel before sharing information.

Slow down

Attackers rely on urgency. Pause and think before acting on unexpected requests.

Use multi-factor authentication

MFA stops credential-based attacks even if a password is compromised.

Report suspicious contact

Notify your IT/security team immediately if you receive suspicious communications.
